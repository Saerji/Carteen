# Carteen: A Java-Based Canteen Cart and Order History System

*Team Ooptimus Prime*

Carteen is a Java-based canteen cart and order history system with a graphical user interface. It helps students pre-order food from the school canteen by choosing a shop, adding products to a cart, checking out, and reviewing their past orders.

## Overview

Waiting in line at the canteen takes up break time, and students often do not know the total cost of their meal until they reach the counter. Carteen lets students prepare an order ahead of time, see the total price as they add items, and look back at what they ordered before.

## Features

- Browse the available canteen shops
- View each shop's products with their prices
- Add products to the cart with a chosen quantity
- See the cart with per-item subtotals and the total price
- Remove items from the cart
- Check out to place an order
- View order history with the date, items, and total of each order
- Input validation with clear messages for wrong entries

## Planned Features

- Login system with a separate order history for each account
- Budget tracker based on the cart total
- Saving orders so history is kept after closing the system
